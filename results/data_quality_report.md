## Known data caveats

- The codebook warns that the `SALE` flag is not set consistently, so a promotion is also flagged when the price is >= 5% below the regular price.
- `SALE = 'G'` appears in the data but is not documented in the codebook; it is kept as its own promotion type.
- `PROFIT` is based on average acquisition cost, so margins around manufacturer deals can be distorted (codebook Part 4).
- The codebook places Easter 1990 in week 28; the actual date (15 Apr 1990) is in week 31, which is what the calendar uses.
